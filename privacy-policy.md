# Privacy Policy — BuloCheck

BuloCheck is a Chrome extension developed as a Final Degree Project (TFG).

## Data collected
- Email address and password for user authentication
- Title and body of news articles submitted for analysis

## How data is used
- Email and password are used exclusively to create and authenticate user accounts
- Article content is sent to an AI model to determine whether the news is real or fake
- Analysis results are stored in a private database associated with the user account

## Third parties
- Article content is processed by a machine learning model hosted on HuggingFace Spaces
- No data is sold or transferred to third parties for purposes unrelated to fake news detection
